O.B. Montessori Center

A DIAMOND LEGACY OF LEARNING


Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC) in the Philippines is a progressive, co-educational school that offers Pre-K to 12, serving as an enriching alternative to international schools.


Through Dr. Maria Montessori's holistic approach to education, we prepare every child for life and independence.


We aim to transform our students into self-developing, productive individuals empowered to succeed, lead, and shape an ever-changing world.


Feel free to explore our programs and discover a community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to grow and develop!
