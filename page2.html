Academic Programs


Casa

(Pre-Kindergarten to Kindergarten)

The Casa Program encourages sensory exploration, independence, and learning. It aims to develop the transformation of children from ages 3 to 5 while consciously and willingly learning from their school environment. This is where students form their personalities and develop their will and intelligence.



Primary Grade School

(Grades 1 to 3)

The Primary Grade School Program aids in the intellectual and physicl development on students from ages 6 to 8. It focuses on foundational academics using Montessori materials and structured environments. As they relate to the world, they grow to achieve independence from adults and gain confidence.



Intermediate Grade School

(Grades 4 to 6)

In the Intermediate Grade School Program, students deepen their knowledge about their universal responsibility to care for their life and environment from ages 9 to 12. This aims to build academic proficiency, environmental awareness, teamwork, and leadership.



Junior High School

(Grades 7 to 10)

Our Junior High School Program prepares students for adult responsibilities at ages 13 to 16. This focuses on strengthening problem-solving, collaboration, and communication as they begin to seek economic independence.



Senior High School

(Grades 11 to 12)

The Senior High School Program further prepares students ages 17 and 18 with the skills and knowledge necessary for future success. The GAS Plus curriculum is designed to provide flexibility in exploring new career options and moving up to college.
